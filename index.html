Study Your Way

Four ways to study at TEAM — choose the one that fits your life.

Day

Monday to Friday

Full-time study on the Mengo campus, for school leavers and anyone free in the day.

Evening

After work

Keep your job and study in the evening on the same programmes and the same campus.

Weekend

Saturday and Sunday

Popular with working professionals and students travelling in from outside Kampala.

ODeL

Open Distance and e-Learning

Study from anywhere, with materials and assessment online through the e-Learning platform.
